## Phase 1: what exists

- **Theme:** `src/theme/tokens.ts` feeds both Tailwind (`tailwind.config.ts`) and `ThemeProvider` (CSS variables via NativeWind `vars()`). System, light and dark modes, with the choice persisted through the expo-sqlite kv-store (synchronous, so there's no flash on launch). Native chrome follows via `Appearance.setColorScheme`. - **Database:** Drizzle schema for all 11 spec tables, plus `latitude`/`longitude` on trips, `quantity` on trip items, `locked` on outfit items, and `archivedNote`, `category` and `colors` on wishlist. Every table has a UUID primary key, createdAt/updatedAt and a soft-delete `deletedAt`. Migrations are generated by drizzle-kit and applied on launch by `DatabaseGate`. Live queries are on (`enableChangeListener`).
- **Seed:** 36 garments (2 in Storage) plus 3 wishlist entries, with placeholder cutouts (WebP with alpha, 1200px plus a 400px thumb) rendered from the prototype silhouettes. Also 6 outfits, 4 planned days, a Lisbon trip with a packing list, and the statuses worn ×3, laundry ×3, lent ×2 (Nadia, Jo), dry cleaner ×1 and storage ×2.
- **Placeholder screens** read real data: Today shows status tiles, Wardrobe shows zones with thumbnails and empty hangers for pieces that are out, Planner shows upcoming plans, and Me has settings plus "Reset demo data" in a sheet.

## Phase 3: what exists

- **Capture (`/add`, full-screen modal, always dark):** `expo-camera` preview with a dashed garment guide (Hanger or Flat lay), corner brackets, flash, flip and a shutter with a white flash. The camera permission is asked for on the first add only, behind a branded explainer card; once refused, the card links to Settings; without a camera (the Simulator) it falls back to photos. The gallery (`expo-image-picker`) takes up to 10 photos at once, which become a batch ("2 of 4") with Skip.
- **Background removal (`features/add/segmentation`):** one call, `removeBackground(photo)` → `{ cutoutUri, width, height, bounds, engine }`. On device by default through our local module `modules/subject-segmentation`:
  - iOS 17+: Vision's foreground instance mask
  - Android: ML Kit Subject Segmentation, whose model Play services downloads on first use. The module checks for it and downloads it with progress, which the review screen shows as "Preparing on-device cutouts · 42%".

  The module returns a PNG with alpha, cropped to the garment plus 4%, with orientation corrected, and reports where the garment sat in the photo. A remove.bg adapter takes over when on-device removal isn't available, but only if `EXPO_PUBLIC_REMOVE_BG_KEY` is set (dev only; see decision 32). The eraser/restore brush is a typed stub (`segmentation/refine.ts`) and its button stays hidden.

- **Magic cutout (DESIGN.md #3):** a diagonal shimmer sweeps every 1.25s while it works (at least 1.1s, so it reads). Then the photo blurs, scales 1.06 and dissolves over 1.1s, a checkerboard flashes, and the garment lifts −8px with a spring before settling in the middle. Detected colour chips pop in 120ms apart. "Keep original" plays it backwards (and is forced on when there's no cutout). Failures explain themselves, with Try again or keep the photo.
- **Colours (`lib/color-extract.ts`):** the cutout is read with Skia at about 64px (`readPixels`, unpremultiplied). Pixels with alpha under 128 are dropped, then k-means (k ≤ 3) runs in CIELAB from a deterministic start. Shades closer than ΔE 10 merge, clusters under 8% are dropped, and each is named by its nearest `FASHION_PALETTE` colour using CIEDE2000. Without a cutout, the middle of the photo is sampled.
- **Category:** `classifyGarment()` is pluggable (`setGarmentClassifier`). Nothing is plugged in, so it returns null and a quick-pick sheet of large category tiles opens. A confident guess (≥ 0.75) would skip the sheet; a weak one is pre-highlighted.
- **Details form (`features/items/details`), shared by Add and Edit:**
  - name: suggested ("Camel wool coat") until you type your own
  - chips: category, kind, colours (detected first, up to 3), pattern, materials (which edit a free-text composition), seasons, occasions, size (per category, plus free text)
  - text fields: brand (with suggestions from your wardrobe), price with a currency chip, store, care notes, tags (with suggestions)
  - purchased: a month and year chip picker

  Validation shows after the first save attempt. Item detail → Edit opens the same form as a modal (`/edit-item/[id]`); a new category moves the piece as in phase 2.

- **Save:** the photo is first made an upright, ≤2048px JPEG working copy. Saving writes `documents/items/<id>/original.jpg`, `cutout.webp` (1200px) and `thumb.webp` (400px), both WebP with alpha via `expo-image-manipulator`. The piece goes into its category's default zone in the wardrobe on show (never into storage), with the `itemAdded` haptic.
- **Fly-in (DESIGN.md #4):** the cutout leaves the review stage at 3.1×, lifts (−21px, 3.3×, −2°), overshoots its slot (+10px, 0.9×, 5°) and settles at 1.35s. This happens over a miniature of its zone, alongside the newest neighbours. The hanger pops in, a ring pulses, the landed hanger swings −5° → 3.5° → −1.5°, and the neighbours nudge. Then come "Hung on the rail." (or shelf, drawer, rack, tray), what was filed where, and Add another / View in wardrobe (Next photo while a batch is going).
- **Tests (235):** LAB conversion, CIEDE2000 against Sharma's published pairs, k-means and cluster filtering on synthetic pixels, palette naming, draft defaults, suggested names, price parsing, validation and the form's round trip, image paths and sizes, the classify fallback, engine choice and bounds, the flow reducer, the new-piece wardrobe rule and the saved copy.

## Phase 5: what exists

**Audit at the start:** `useOutfits` (list, an item's outfits, the picker) and `matches.ts` were done and are reused. `mutations` was partial (membership and favourite only). `OutfitPreview` drew approximate mini boards that no builder could reproduce. The builder, shuffle, snapshots and saving were missing.

- **Route:** `/outfit/[id]` is the builder. `new` starts a board; `?item=<id>` (item detail → Add to outfit → New outfit) and `?items=<id>,<id>` (for phase 6's suggestion Edit) preload pieces in their role's spot. Any other id opens that outfit with its layout restored. It's a full-screen modal with no swipe-to-dismiss, so board drags can't close it; closing with unsaved changes asks first, and Android Back closes an open sheet before the builder.
- **Board (`features/outfits/builder`):** a `bg-board` board with a faint 18pt dot grid, drawn in **one Skia canvas** driven by Reanimated shared values: the garments (each with its alpha-mask shadow), snap guides and the dashed selection outline. The board keeps the prototype's 366 × 476 shape and fits between the header and the tray.
  - **Gestures:** one composed gesture on the board, `Race(Simultaneous(Pan, Pinch, Rotation), Tap)`, all on the UI thread. Tap selects (or clears). Pan moves the piece under the finger, and the grabbed piece lifts 4%. Pinch and rotation act on the piece under the fingers, else the selected one, and run together with pan. Each gesture ends in one commit, so one undo step.
  - **Corner handle:** the accent circle at the outline's turned corner. Dragging it scales with distance and rotates with angle around the piece's centre (scale 0.35–2.6). Rotation settles on 0°/±90°/180° within 4°, with a tick.
  - **Snap guides:** to the board's centre lines and to other pieces' centres and edges, within 8pt, with a light haptic each time a new guide appears. A dragged piece's centre can't leave the board.
  - **Trash:** a bin rises from the board's foot while dragging (bouncy spring). Over it, it swells to 1.22 and turns red, the piece dims to 40% and the hint reads "Release to remove". Dropping it shrinks the piece into the bin with the `dropSuccess` haptic.
  - **Floating toolbar** (hidden while dragging): lock, bring forward, send backward (one step each, dimmed at the ends of the stack) and remove. Locked pieces wear a small upright lock badge.
  - **Undo:** a header button with a 30-step stack (add, remove, move, scale/rotate, lock, z-order, shuffle).
  - **Pops:** pieces from the tray or a shuffle pop in from half size and ±10° on the bouncy spring (prototype popA/popB); a saved outfit's pieces fade in. Under Reduce Motion everything is a short fade.
  - **Accessibility:** the canvas is drawn, so each piece also has an invisible element at its bounds, labelled with its name and lock, with actions: select, lock, bring forward, send backward, make larger or smaller, rotate, and remove.
- **Garment bounds:** each cutout is sampled at 64px once to find its opaque bounds (`bounds.ts`). Outlines, snapping and hit tests hug the garment, not the image, which matters for the demo cutouts and their transparent margins. Pieces get at least a 44pt touch target.
- **Tray:** category tabs (Tops, Bottoms, Layers, Dresses, Shoes, Accessories) with counts, over a horizontal FlashList of in-wardrobe pieces only. Tap a piece to place it in its role's flat-lay spot (stepping aside if the spot's taken), or drag it up and drop it anywhere on the board (the board rings in accent while you're over it). A piece already on the board shows a green tick; tapping it selects it, dropping it moves it.
- **Shuffle and lock:** Shuffle swaps every unlocked piece for another available piece in the same role. It favours colours that harmonise with what stays (`colorHarmony` from `matches.ts`) and shared seasons, choosing at random among the best few so each shuffle differs. It never puts a garment on the board twice, and a piece with nothing to swap in stays. Swapped pieces pop in. On an empty board, Shuffle (or "Shuffle a look") builds a starter outfit. When everything is locked, or nothing could swap, a toast says so.
- **Save:** a sheet with the name (the placeholder suggests one from the colours, "Ecru & navy"; a blank name takes it), occasion and seasons, pre-filled from what the pieces share. Saving writes the outfit and its `outfit_items` (normalised `x`, `y`, `scale`, `rotation`, `zIndex`, `locked`; links taken off are soft-deleted, links put back are revived) and fires `outfitSaved`. The sheet then turns into the prototype's saved card, with the snapshot popping in, **Plan it** and **View outfits**. Saving again updates the same outfit.
- **Snapshots (`snapshots.tsx`):** drawn offscreen with Skia's `drawAsImage`, using the same `OutfitScene` component the list draws live. They're cropped to the outfit's content frame and saved as transparent 600px PNGs at `documents/outfits/<id>-<version>.png`, with older versions deleted. A single queue renders them one at a time: a save jumps the queue, and lists render missing ones in the background. Seeded outfits have none until they're first shown. Saving an edit re-renders the snapshot, and "Add to outfit" on item detail clears it so it's drawn afresh. Reset demo data removes the folder.
- **Outfits list:** a masonry FlashList of snapshot cards whose height follows each outfit's frame. Until a snapshot exists, the card draws the scene live and keeps it until the image has loaded, so nothing blinks. Filter chips are All, Favourites, the occasions in use and the seasons in use; they combine, and all-year outfits match any season. The heart toggles favourite. **Hold a card** for Edit, Plan for a day, Duplicate ("… copy") and Delete (asks once; soft delete). Screen readers get the same menu as an action.
- **Plan for a day:** a `DatePickerSheet` with Today, Tomorrow, In 2 days, a week and 2 weeks. It writes `planned_outfits` (one outfit per day, replacing and naming the one it replaced), so plans show in the Planner tab now. Phase 6 builds the calendar on top. Deleting an outfit drops its upcoming plans, and past wear logs keep it.
- **Elsewhere:** item detail's "In N outfits" row shows the same thumbnails. The Planner hides plans for deleted outfits. `samplePixels` now returns its dimensions.
- **Tests (349):** normalised ↔ canvas round trips, hit testing (rotation, 44pt minimum), the handle, rotation settling, snap detection (centre lines, centres, edges, nearest wins), placement, the content frame, z-order (renumbering, one step, the ends, add and remove), the undo stack, shuffle (locks, availability, roles, no duplicates, harmony, starter outfits), opaque bounds, save defaults and copy names, list filters and plan copy.

## Phase 6: what exists

**Audit at the start:** `wearCache.ts` was done and is reused. `wearLog.ts` was partial: it logged or unlogged one piece for today, with no past days, outfit, selfie, weather, note or auto-laundry. `items/matches.ts` was partial, with hard-coded weights. Weather, the engine, Today's carousel and nudges, the calendar, the dock, the log sheet, auto-laundry and the reminder were missing.

- **Weather (`features/weather`):**
  - **Where:** the first time weather shows, the Today card explains why location helps and offers **Use my location** (the only place permission is asked) or **Choose a city**. The city sheet searches Open-Meteo geocoding. The choice is a preference (`weatherPlace`: device or a city) and can be changed by tapping the card or from Me → Settings → Weather. If location is refused, the card offers a city instead, plus Open Settings once it's blocked.
  - **Forecast:** Open-Meteo, no key. One request gets current temperature, feels-like, WMO code, chance of rain and day/night, plus daily highs, lows, feels-like and rain chance from 7 days back to 16 ahead, in the place's own days. `fetchDailyRange()` is exposed for phase 7's trips.
  - **Cache:** the last forecast is kept in the kv-store and shown at once and offline. It refreshes when a screen comes into view or the app returns, if it's over 30 minutes old. After an hour the card says "Updated 3h ago", plus "offline" when a refresh failed. A Skeleton shows while there's nothing yet.
  - **Units:** everything is °C inside; `formatTemp` applies the °C/°F preference on screen. WMO codes map to a summary and a lucide icon (`wmo.ts`), with the moon at night.
- **Suggestion engine (`features/suggestions`, pure, every weight in `config.ts`):**
  1. **Layers from the weather:** hot (feels-like ≥ 25°) adds none; mild adds a light layer (a light jacket, trench or cardigan); cold (< 12°) adds outerwear (warm coats first) plus knitwear, as the top or over it. Rain (a wet WMO code or ≥ 50%) favours waterproof layers and shoes and marks down suede, canvas or knit shoes. In hot rain, a light waterproof layer is optional.
  2. **Candidates:** top + bottoms + shoes, or dress + shoes, plus the layers and an accessory, from pieces in the wardrobe only (never basics). The best few per slot go into the combinations, so 500 garments stay fast; the demo wardrobe takes 5–15 ms.
  3. **Scores:**
     - colour harmony in HSL: neutrals pair with everything; analogous and complementary hues score up; saturated hues a third of the wheel apart clash; more than two accent hues is marked down
     - season (flipped south of the equator) and occasion: a given one, or agreement between the pieces
     - freshness: a penalty that fades over the 7 days after a wear, and a boost from 21 to 90 idle days plus a nudge for below-median wear counts
     - a favourites bonus
     - weather fit: knitwear sits out hot days, boots lose points in the heat, summer-only pieces lose points in the cold
  4. **Diverse picks:** no two outfits shown together share more than one piece, and each card leads with a different dress or top when the wardrobe allows.

  It's deterministic: the seed is the day, and each piece's small nudge is keyed by its id, so input order doesn't matter. Shuffle takes the next-best look that's diverse against the other cards and not yet shown on that card, then cycles.

- **Find matches** (item detail) now runs on the engine's harmony and config (`suggestions/matches.ts`), with a small bonus for a shared occasion. The builder's Shuffle uses the same harmony.
- **Today (`features/today`, Today.dc.html):**
  - the italic date, greeting, theme toggle and the weather card
  - **three outfit cards** in a snapping carousel: a tinted board drawn live with Skia, tags ("Fits 18°", then Rain-ready, Under-worn, Favourite, Fresh picks or Light layer), "Pick 1 of 3", a name (the saved outfit's, or one from its colours) and a line such as "Work · light layer for 24°"
    - **Wear this** logs the pieces with today's weather. Its toast counts what went to the basket. The board gets a "Worn today" badge and the button becomes **Logged**; tapping that undoes the log exactly.
    - **Shuffle** pops a new look in.
    - **Edit** opens the builder: the saved outfit, or `/outfit/new?items=…`.
  - Today's plan, if any, leads the carousel as "Planned for today". A card whose pieces go out elsewhere (into the wash, lent) is replaced on its own; a worn one stays.
  - **Tiles:** laundry, lent and forgotten. Forgotten opens the Wardrobe grid sorted least-worn until Declutter exists.
  - **Wear it again:** one idle piece that suits the day (not one already on the cards), the engine's pairings, and **Plan for tomorrow**, which turns into "Planned · Fri 23" and back.
  - **Upcoming trip:** "Lisbon · in 11 days" with packing progress. It opens the Planner on the trip's first day until packing lists exist.
- **Planner (`features/planner`, Planner.dc.html):**
  - **Calendar:** a week strip that springs open into the month (drawer spring), keeping the selected week in place. Days show worn ticks, up to three colour dots for a plan (top to toe), a trip bar, a today ring and the selected day inverted. Arrows step weeks when folded and months when open.
  - **The selected day:** an eyebrow (Today, Planned, Free day, Past, Outfit journal or Worn today), the date, and the weather: the log's for a logged day, otherwise the forecast. During a trip it shows "Lisbon trip · Day 2 of 5".
    - **Planned:** the outfit's board. Today offers **Log as worn** and Swap; a future day **Swap outfit** and Clear; a past day Log as worn and Clear.
    - **Free:** **Suggest one** (the engine with that day's forecast) or **Pick an outfit**.
    - **Past and empty:** **Log an outfit** (pick one, or "Pick pieces instead").
    - **Journal:** an arched frame with the mirror selfie (or the pieces), what it was, the note, the pieces and **Edit log**.
  - **Dock:** the saved outfits on frosted glass above the tab bar, favourites first. Tap one to plan it on the selected day, or drag it up onto any day. The day under the finger swells with an accent ring and a tick; dropping plans it with `dropSuccess`. Past days refuse with "That day has passed".
  - **Log sheet:** tick the outfit's pieces, **Add a piece** (a picker stacked on top), a mirror selfie (camera; photos where there's no camera), a note and the day's weather. A caption says which pieces will go straight to the laundry. Editing a log can also delete it, after a confirmation.
- **Wear logging (`planner/wearLog.ts`):** one log per day. `writeWearLog` can add pieces (Today, Wear today on detail), remove them, or replace the whole set (the log sheet), and returns a receipt that `undoWearLog` reverses exactly. Every change recomputes `wearCount` and `lastWornAt`.
  - **Statuses:** only today's log moves pieces: to worn, or straight to the basket when auto-laundry says so, always through the transition table. Logging a past day counts the wear only.
  - **Un-logging today:** a worn piece goes back on its hanger; one in the basket stays there. Deleting a log also deletes its selfie.
- **Auto-laundry:** a piece goes to the basket once its wear days since its last wash reach the category's setting. `items.washed_at` is stamped whenever a piece goes into the wash or to the cleaner (migration `0002_items_washed_at`; `0003_backfill_washed_at` gives existing pieces a last wash from their state and last wear, so their old history doesn't count). Me → Settings → **Auto-laundry** has a stepper per category (never, or 1–8 wears) and a reset.
- **Plan reminder (optional):** Me → Settings → **Plan reminder** (asks for notification permission the first time). It fires at 20:00 on evenings when the next day has nothing planned. A week is scheduled ahead and resynced whenever plans change; tapping it opens the Planner on that day.
- **Dev:** a thermometer button on Today, or holding the weather card, overrides the feels-like temperature (−2° to 33°) and rain for the session (development builds only).
- **Seed:** wear logs carry generated weather from a separate random stream, so the history is unchanged, which was checked against the committed version. `washedAt` is set so clean pieces start at zero wears. Existing installs get the weather after Me → Reset demo data.
- **Shared pieces:** a `PopIn` primitive, `SearchField inSheet`, and `OutfitPiece` now carries category and colours. `placeLook` lays out a whole look (knitwear worn over a dress or top takes the layer spot) for the cards, the journal, the builder's preload and saved suggestions. `LookBoard` draws a look that isn't a saved outfit.
- **Tests (416):**
  - **engine:** a test for each rule: bands, rain, harmony, season, occasion, freshness, favourites, diversity, Shuffle, determinism, input order and Wear it again
  - **weather:** WMO mapping, units, Open-Meteo parsing, cache age, day snapshots and matching the cache to the place
  - **wear logging:** the auto-laundry rule, wash stamps, log effects, cache maths on log and undo, and the copy
  - **Today and Planner:** the carousel, calendar rows, week stepping, day kinds, dots, labels and trips, and reminder timing
  - **layout and seed:** `placeLook`, and seed weather and wash dates
- **Checked in the iOS Simulator** (iPhone 17 Pro, light and dark):
  - **Today:** the explainer; live Lisbon weather (20°, mainly clear; the city was set in the app's storage because the location prompt can't be answered headless); three diverse cards; the tiles; Wear it again; the trip card
  - **Planner:** today's journal, a planned day and a free day with its forecast
  - **Migration:** `0002` ran on launch

  Taps, drags and sheets couldn't be driven headless, so **Wear this, Shuffle, Edit, the dock drag, Suggest, the log sheet, the selfie camera, the location prompt and the reminder were not exercised by hand.**

## Decisions

1. **Routes live in `src/app`.** That's the SDK 57 template default and what `AGENTS.md` expects. Everything else follows the spec's feature folders under `src/`.
2. **Contrast fixes.** Muted text is `#6E6860`, the spec's `#8A847C` is kept as `faint`, and filled accent buttons use `#C2412C`. Details are in DESIGN.md.
3. **Styling.** NativeWind 4.2 with Tailwind 3.4. NativeWind 5 is still a release candidate.
4. **Theming through CSS variables** set by ThemeProvider, so no `dark:` variants are needed anywhere.
5. **Seed cutouts** are WebP with alpha (about 25KB each, against about 800KB as PNG). The silhouette shadow is drawn at runtime from the alpha mask.
6. **Demo data loads automatically on first launch** until onboarding exists. Onboarding will then offer "Load demo wardrobe" or "Start empty".
7. **Day keys:** wear logs and plans store the local calendar day as `"YYYY-MM-DD"` text to avoid timezone drift.
8. **Join tables also get UUIDs and timestamps** (for sync tombstones), with unique indexes on their natural keys.
9. **Tab bar blur:** iOS uses expo-blur. Android uses an opaque glass fill, because Android blur needs a `BlurTargetView` around the content.
10. **Preferences** (theme, units, currency, reduce motion) live in a persisted Zustand store backed by `expo-sqlite/kv-store`. No AsyncStorage dependency.
11. **Added `expo-blur`.** It isn't in the spec's stack list; it's used for the frosted tab bar.
12. **Bundle ID is `com.chamithwijesooriya.mycloset`** on iOS and Android.
13. **`plugins/withoutPushEntitlement.js` strips the iOS push capability** that expo-notifications adds, so a free Personal Team can sign the app. Reminders are local notifications only. It must stay listed before `expo-notifications` in `app.json`.
14. **Wardrobe direction A** (stacked zones) is built. B and C stay as prototypes.
15. **Detail transition is a custom overlay.** Reanimated 4.5.1's shared element transitions sit behind a static feature flag that is off by default (`ENABLE_SHARED_ELEMENT_TRANSITIONS`), so they are still experimental. `/item/[id]` is a `transparentModal` with `animation: 'none'`. The screen flies a hero cutout from the measured thumbnail to the stage on a spring (pure geometry in `detail/hero.ts`) while the tint fades in and the card slides up. The tapped piece is hidden in its list meanwhile. On close it flies back if it's still the same piece in the same wardrobe and isn't zoomed or flipped; otherwise the page fades. Under Reduce Motion everything is a 200ms fade.
16. **Closet tap opens the quick sheet, Grid tap opens detail**, as in the prototypes. From the quick sheet, the hero leaves the sheet's thumbnail and returns to the piece's spot in the closet.
17. **Item detail hosts its own `BottomSheetModalProvider`.** A transparent modal is a separate native presentation on iOS, so sheets from the root provider would open behind it.
18. **The toast host is mounted in the root layout.** It never was, so the existing toasts were silent. On iOS it sits in a `FullWindowOverlay` so toasts also show above modals.
19. **One sort for both views.** The Closet orders each zone by it, so colour sort gives a rainbow rail. The sort control now shows in the Closet too.
20. **Colour order:** neutrals first, light to dark; then hues from 340° round the wheel, so burgundy and red sit together; pieces with no colour last.
21. **Cost per wear** is price ÷ max(1, wears): an unworn piece counts as one wear, so it shows its full price. Free pieces sort first; unpriced pieces sort last and show "—".
22. **Editing the category** moves a piece to the new category's zone only if it was in its old category's kind of zone. A piece you dragged somewhere else stays put.
23. **"+ New" wardrobe** offers Wardrobe (Home's zones) or Storage (archive icon, storage zones, pieces count as stored). Names are unique, ignoring case, and capped at 32 characters.
24. **"Add to outfit"** keeps the working membership sheet (add or remove the piece from saved outfits) and adds a "New outfit" row to the `/outfit/new` placeholder.
25. **Find matches** draws from every wardrobe (stored and out pieces score lower). Tapping a match switches the detail to it in place.
26. **Demo originals are rendered, not photographed.** About 14KB each, roughly 0.5MB in all. Existing installs need Me → Reset demo data to get them. Re-running `npm run assets` re-encodes every image with slightly different bytes, so when only new files are wanted, restore the committed cutouts and icons afterwards.
27. **Background removal is our own local Expo module**, `modules/subject-segmentation` (about 250 lines of Swift and Kotlin), rather than a library. It's autolinked from `modules/`. It gives us cropped, orientation-corrected output with bounds, and a real model-download state on Android. The libraries researched were `@six33/react-native-bg-removal` (no model check, last released January 2026) and `@rbayuokt/expo-background-removal` (three weeks old, one author).
28. **iOS deployment target is 17.0** (via `expo-build-properties`), so Vision's foreground mask is always available. The only iPhones lost are those that stop at iOS 16 (iPhone 8 and X).
29. **Onboarding stays in phase 8.**
30. **Purchase date is a chip month picker**, not `@react-native-community/datetimepicker`: the app shows months only ("Aug 2025"), chips match the form, and it needs no native dependency.
31. **No install-time ML Kit model hint on Android.** expo-camera already declares `com.google.mlkit.vision.DEPENDENCIES` (`barcode_ui`), and a second value would break the manifest merge. The model downloads on first use instead, with progress.
32. **remove.bg is a dev-only fallback.** `EXPO_PUBLIC_*` values are inlined into the JS bundle, so the key can be extracted from any build. It's meant for testing in the Simulator, where Vision doesn't run. A production fallback must go through a server.
33. **Cutouts and thumbnails are WebP with alpha**; originals are JPEG. `expo-image-manipulator` 57 keeps alpha on both platforms (iOS resizes with `opaque = false` and encodes via SDWebImageWebPCoder; Android uses ARGB bitmaps).
34. **Stored colours use the palette hex**, so filters and swatches stay consistent. The measured colour is shown on the review chips only.
35. **A new piece goes to the wardrobe on show**, or Home if that's a storage wardrobe, since a new piece is in use rather than stored.
36. **Details is a full screen, not a bottom sheet**, as in the prototype. It's long and has text fields, and a full screen handles the keyboard better. Edit presents the same form as a modal page sheet.
37. **The flight lands in a miniature of the zone** on the saved screen (as in the prototype), not in the live closet behind the modal. "View in wardrobe" then opens that wardrobe's closet with filters cleared, where the new piece sorts first.
38. **`SchemeScope`** (in ThemeProvider) renders a subtree in a fixed scheme. The camera uses it to stay dark in light mode without hard-coded colours.

39. **Transition table.** A piece that isn't at hand can't be worn: one in the wash or at the cleaner can't be lent or stored, and a lent one can't be stored. Archived pieces don't change, archiving needs a reason, and "In storage" needs a storage wardrobe. Everything else is allowed, including lent → laundry (it came back dirty). Refused chips are dimmed but still tappable, so they can say why. A wear logged for a piece in the wash keeps the wear and leaves its status alone.
40. **Storage status ↔ storage wardrobes:** the brief's proposal, as built in phase 2.
    - Moving a piece into a storage-type wardrobe sets `storage`, and moving it out sets `in_wardrobe`.
    - "In storage" moves the piece into the first storage wardrobe. Any other status from storage brings it back to _home_, which is now defined as the first everyday wardrobe in the user's order.
    - Storage-type is still the archive icon (decision 23), so there's no new column.
41. **`statusChangedAt` is generic** rather than a dry-cleaner-only drop-off date. It also orders "Just worn" and the basket. `remindAt` and `readyAt` are separate, explicit columns.
42. **Reminder ids are derived, not stored:** `lend-reminder:<itemId>`. Only `remindAt` is persisted (as the toggle's state), and reminders fire at 10:00 local time.
43. **No exact-alarm permission on Android.** expo-notifications falls back to an inexact alarm (a few minutes' drift), which suits a reminder and avoids a Play policy declaration. `app.json` is unchanged and `withoutPushEntitlement` still sits ahead of expo-notifications.
44. **The dry cleaner's ready day is optional** and set from a chip (the prototype shows one). The progress bar appears only once there's a ready day.
45. **The closet basket counts laundry across all wardrobes.** Pieces that are out (empty hangers) can't be dragged. Worn pieces reach the basket from Laundry → Just worn, or from the quick sheet's "Send to laundry".
46. **Wash done writes first, then animates.** The update runs immediately (so leaving mid-flight loses nothing) while a snapshot of the pile flies out.
47. **The Laundry screen is a pushed route**, `/laundry?tab=laundry|lent|cleaner`, rather than living inside the Me tab. Then the basket, Today and Me can all open it, and Back returns to wherever you came from.
48. **"Lend something" shows the first 48 matching pieces plus a search field**, not a virtualised list inside the sheet.
49. **Wardrobe management:**
    - The last wardrobe can't be deleted, nor can the last everyday one: stored pieces need somewhere to return to. For the same reason, the last everyday wardrobe can't become storage.
    - A deleted wardrobe's zones are soft-deleted. Its pieces move to the chosen wardrobe (by default one of the same kind); anything storage refuses goes home.
50. **Grid multi-select:**
    - Long-press starts it, taps toggle, and deselecting the last piece ends it.
    - A selection belongs to one view of one wardrobe, so switching either drops it. Android Back clears it.
    - Bulk moves skip refused pieces and say how many stayed put.
51. **New primitives:** `DatePickerSheet`, count badges on `Segmented`, and `Sheet.stackBehavior`. The date picker opens on top of the sheet it came from.
52. **`wicker`, `wickerShade` and `wickerDeep` tokens** (illustration only, so they have no contrast pairs) colour the basket in both themes.
53. **The board is one Skia canvas**, not a view per piece. Skia redraws each garment from its source image at the current scale and angle, so pieces stay crisp at 2.6×, and every transform runs on the UI thread from shared values. Each piece gets an invisible element with actions for screen readers.
54. **Board coordinates:** a fixed 1 : 1.3 board (the prototype's 366 × 476), so a layout restores exactly on any screen. `x` and `y` are the garment's centre as a fraction of width and height, `rotation` is in degrees, and at `scale` 1 a garment's longest visible side is 40% of the board's width. Accessories default to 0.7 and shoes to 0.9.
55. **Snapshots are drawn offscreen with Skia** (`drawAsImage`), rather than captured with `makeImageFromView` or `react-native-view-shot`. That means no selection chrome to hide, it works for outfits that were never opened (seeded ones render lazily), and there's no new native dependency. They're transparent PNGs carrying the light theme's warm shadow; each card draws its own tinted ground, so they suit both themes.
56. **Snapshot files are versioned**, `documents/outfits/<id>-<version>.png` rather than `<id>.png`. Image caches key on the URI, so an edited outfit would otherwise keep showing its old board. Older versions are deleted after each render.
57. **Thumbnails are cropped to the outfit's content frame**: pieces plus a margin, between 0.9 and 1.3 times as tall as wide. Each piece counts as a square of its longest side, so a card's height is known before any image loads and the masonry never jumps.
58. **Lock means "keep for Shuffle"** (the prototype's "Lock piece for shuffle"). Locked pieces can still be moved.
59. **Bring forward / Send backward move one step**, as in design tools, rather than straight to the front or back as in the prototype. With a handful of pieces, it's easier to place one exactly.
60. **Snapping** matches centre to centre (the board's centre lines and other pieces) and edge to edge, within 8pt. Rotation settles on right angles within 4°.
61. **Undo keeps 30 steps.** The brief asked for at most one; a stack costs nothing extra. Opening an outfit starts a fresh stack.
62. **Pieces land in role spots** (a layer left, the top centre, bottoms right, shoes and bag below), shared with the demo seed. Shoes and accessories sit at 0.72, the prototype's height (they were at 0.8). Existing installs keep the older demo layout until Me → Reset demo data.
63. **Shuffle on an empty board builds a starter outfit**, and Shuffle also replaces unlocked pieces that are out (worn, washing, lent).
64. **The tray offers only pieces hanging in the wardrobe**, without basics (underwear, socks, activewear). An outfit can still contain pieces that are out; they stay on its board.
65. **The builder is a full-screen modal with its own `BottomSheetModalProvider`** (decision 17's reason), with no swipe-to-dismiss.
66. **One outfit per planned day:** planning replaces what was there and says what it replaced. Deleting an outfit removes its upcoming plans but leaves wear logs alone.
67. **The builder draws full cutouts** (crisp when scaled up), with the thumbnail standing in while the cutout decodes. Snapshots and live thumbnails use the 400px thumbnails.

68. **Weather is °C inside.** Only the display converts, so the engine's bands and the cache don't depend on the unit.
69. **The weather place is a preference.** It's the device or a chosen city, and stays empty until the explainer is answered. Permission is asked only from "Use my location"; the city search is Open-Meteo geocoding.
70. **One forecast request** covers current conditions plus 7 days back and 16 ahead, in the place's own days. It's cached in the kv-store, refreshed when shown if it's over 30 minutes old, and its age shows after an hour.
71. **Bands:** hot from 25° feels-like, cold below 12°, mild between. The prototype puts a light layer at 24°, hence 25. Rain means a wet WMO code or at least a 50% chance.
72. **Which temperature:** Today uses the current feels-like, which is what the card shows, with the day's highest chance of rain. Other days use a daytime feel of 0.65 × the high + 0.35 × the low.
73. **Garments are read by their words.** Warmth, waterproofing and rain-shy shoes come from the category plus kind, material, name and tags ("trench", "gabardine", "boots", "suede", "down"). Wool alone doesn't make a jacket warm: a wool blazer is still tailoring.
74. **Knitwear sits out hot days** unless it's the only top. A cardigan counts as a light layer; in the cold, any knitwear is the warm layer.
75. **Candidate limits:** the best 6 tops, 6 bottoms, 4 dresses, 4 shoes, 3 layers, 2 knits and 3 accessories; then the best 60 cores; at most 400 ranked looks are kept for Shuffle. Each look is also kept without its accessory, so the diverse pick can drop a shared bag.
76. **Diversity:** no two cards share more than one piece (the spec). Each card also leads with a different dress or top whenever the wardrobe allows.
77. **Seeded by the day.** Suggestions hold all day and change tomorrow, or when the weather band or today's plan changes.
78. **Today's plan leads the carousel** ("Planned for today"), and the engine's picks are diverse against it.
79. **A worn card stays put.** A card whose pieces go out elsewhere is replaced. **Logged** is tappable and undoes exactly: statuses, wash dates and the log's fields.
80. **One wear log per day.** Today and Wear today add to it; the Planner's log sheet replaces its pieces.
81. **Only today's log moves statuses.** Logging last Tuesday counts the wear and updates the cache but leaves pieces where they are now. Un-logging today puts worn pieces back; pieces in the basket stay there.
82. **Auto-laundry counts wear days after `washedAt`**, stamped whenever a piece goes into the wash or to the cleaner. That's the one new column (migration 0002), backfilled for existing pieces (0003). The brief's defaults stand. Categories it didn't list: tops, dresses and shorts after 2; skirts and trousers after 3; outerwear, shoes and accessories never. An undo restores the old wash date.
83. **Planning a suggestion saves it as an outfit**, named from its colours, because plans always point at an outfit. An identical saved outfit is reused. Taking back "Wear it again" deletes an outfit it created.
84. **Suggesting for a future day** treats pieces that are worn or in the wash as available (they'll be clean by then). Plans within 3 days count as wear, so a planned week doesn't repeat itself.
85. **The dock drag starts with an upward pan**, like the builder's tray, so sideways swipes scroll. Hit testing runs on the UI thread from the calendar's measured rect.
86. **Past days can't be planned.** A past day with a plan offers Log as worn; one without offers Log an outfit (pick one, or pieces).
87. **The calendar keeps the whole month grid** and springs its height and offset, so the selected week stays put as it opens. The arrows stay visible when open and step months (the prototype hides them in month view).
88. **The journal's arched frame** shows the selfie, or the pieces laid out when there isn't one. Logs are edited and deleted from the journal; a deleted log's selfie file goes too.
89. **Selfies** are taken with the camera, falling back to photos where there's no camera, and saved as 1400px JPEGs in `documents/logs/`. Ones taken but not kept are deleted when the sheet closes.
90. **The plan reminder is opt-in**, at 20:00, only on evenings before an unplanned day. A week is scheduled at a time and resynced on every plan change.
91. **Interim links:** the Forgotten tile opens the Wardrobe grid sorted least-worn, and the trip card opens the Planner on the trip's first day. Phase 7's Declutter and packing lists will take these over.
92. **Wear it again** prefers a piece that isn't already on Today's cards.
93. **Seed weather comes from its own random stream**, so the generated wear history stays exactly as it was.
94. **`placeLook`** lays out a whole look at once. Knitwear worn over a dress or another top takes the layer's spot instead of covering it. The cards, the journal, the builder's preload and saved suggestions all use it.
95. **Metro's cache is keyed on the migration SQL** (`cacheVersion` in `metro.config.js`). Babel inlines each `.sql` file into `migrations.js`, so without this Metro kept serving drizzle-kit's empty placeholder for 0003 even after restarts, and the app crashed at launch. Now a restart picks up any new or edited migration.

## Open questions

1. **Real garment photos:** the owner plans to upload cutouts and photos. They can now go through the add flow, or replace the placeholders in `assets/seed/` (keep the slugs; originals go in `assets/seed/originals/<slug>.jpg`).
2. **Archived pieces** leave every view. Where should they be listed and restored from? Proposal: Me → Insights or a "Donated & sold" list in phase 7.
3. **Closet tap:** it opens the quick sheet first (as the prototype does) rather than going straight to detail. Say if you'd rather skip the sheet.
4. **Android is untested.** This machine has no Android SDK, so the Kotlin side of `modules/subject-segmentation` has never been compiled. The first `npm run android` is its first build; expect a fix or two there.
5. **A category classifier** could plug into `setGarmentClassifier` later (an on-device image-labelling model, or a server). Worth it?
6. **Strictness of the transition table** (decision 39): for example, a piece in the wash can't be marked as lent. Say if any refusal gets in the way.
7. **Reminder hours:** lend reminders fire at 10:00 and the plan reminder at 20:00. Would you rather pick the times?
8. **Gestures need a hand test.**
   - **Phase 5:** drag, pinch, rotate, the handle, snapping, the trash, dragging from the tray and the save flow.
   - **Phase 6:** Wear this, Logged (undo), Shuffle, Edit, the carousel snapping, the Planner dock drag onto days, Suggest, the log sheet and its piece picker, and the selfie camera.

   These have only been checked in code and unit tests. Please try them on a phone, and on Android when it's built (the "smooth on mid-range Android" check is still open).

9. **Snapshot shadows in dark mode:** they carry the light theme's warm shadow, which is subtle on the dark tint. Rendering a second, dark version is easy if you'd rather have it.
10. **Suggestion feel:** the bands (hot from 25°, cold below 12°) and the weights in `suggestions/config.ts` are tuned on the demo wardrobe. With your own clothes, say if a look feels off (too many forgotten pieces, layers when it's warm), and it's a config change.
11. **Planned suggestions become saved outfits** (decision 83), so planning a few weeks of suggestions adds outfits to the Outfits tab. Keep that, or hide them as "planned looks" until you save one?
12. **Occasion on Today:** the engine takes an occasion, but Today has no picker; it uses the pieces' agreement instead. Add Work / Weekend / Evening chips above the cards?
13. **Location prompt:** the explainer → system prompt → forecast path hasn't been tried on a device (the Simulator check used a city). The first run on your phone is its first real test.
14. **Auto-laundry defaults** for categories the brief didn't list (decision 82). Adjust in Me → Settings, or say and I'll change the defaults.
